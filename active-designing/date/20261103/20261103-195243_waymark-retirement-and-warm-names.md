## What this essay decides

**The waymark system retires, on Keaton's word (`20261103`).** The four-letter SHA3 draws, the
sealed registry, the derivation ceremony, the waymark-ladders rule page, and the ascending-number
rungs -- every part of the system gives way to **warm descriptive strings** drawn fresh from what
the work actually is.

The ascending numbers are already banned by
[`waymark-ascending-numbers.md`](../../../.claude/rules/waymark-ascending-numbers.md), seated Orbit 2
of this journey. This essay retires the rest: the bare four-letter names themselves.

---

## What waymarks gave this tree

**Collision-free identity.** SHA3-256 over a canonical hyphenated input, truncated to a four-letter
word from the `flw-four-letter.txt` list, gave each work ladder a name that could never collide with
another draw. In 33 seated draws across 16 months, zero collisions. The math works.

**A sealed registry.** `construction/waymark-registry.kyri` is SHA3-512-sealed over its body, so no
accidental edit can change a draw after the fact. The witness re-derives every collection mark from
its input on metal. The integrity is real.

**A single grep target.** `grep lotus-audio` finds every mention of the Lotus audio chapter in one command,
across every file and every commit message. Four uppercase letters stand out in prose and in a
terminal.

---

## What waymarks cost this tree

**Clarity, first and hardest.** lotus-audio, glow-language, pixel-glass, compass-surface, compass-identity, harvest-yield, harvest-rest, cion-debride -- a newcomer
meeting any of these words in a header, a commit message, or a module roster learns nothing from
it. The Comlink tendency asks for "the clearest, most fun, safest word -- at whatever length that
word wants to be." A four-letter draw from a word list is the opposite of that test, and only
collision-proofing justified the trade. The question is whether collision-proofing justifies it
enough.

**A registry that must be maintained.** The sealed registry, its SHA3-512 witness, the derivation
witness, the word-list pin, the exclude roster, the hand-seated names -- each is a piece of
infrastructure serving one purpose: naming a work ladder. The waymark-ladders rule page is one of
the longest in `.claude/rules/`, precisely because the system needs that much explanation. A name
should not need a system.

**Ascending numbers that violate stamp-and-name.** Measured by the Orbit 1 census: **41,408**
ascending-number patterns across 21 waymarks, reaching 3,819 files and 3,698 commit messages.
`lotus-audio`, `glow-language`, `pixel-glass` -- each is a forecast wearing a name's clothes, and each stayed
uncorrected the way stamp-and-name line 2 said it would. The ban is already seated; this cost is
already being paid down.

**A vocabulary that crowds out the real names.** `grep lotus-audio` finds the chapter, yet `grep lotus`
finds it better -- because `lotus` says what the chapter builds, and `lotus-audio` says only that
somebody once ran a SHA3 derivation. The four-letter draw was meant to stand beside the real name,
and in practice it stood in front of it: headers read `lotus-audio -- CoreAudio engine` rather than
`CoreAudio engine`. The overhead served the draw, and the reader served the overhead.

---

## The honest question

A waymark without ascending numbers is just a name. And the Comlink tendency already governs names.
The only thing a four-letter draw gives that a warm descriptive string does not is SHA3
collision-proofing. In 33 draws over 16 months this tree experienced zero collisions -- which means
the insurance has been real and the claims have been zero.

The question is whether the premium -- the clarity cost, the registry overhead, the rule-page weight,
the vocabulary crowding -- is worth carrying for an insurance that has never paid out and whose risk
can be managed by a grep.

Keaton's answer: **no.** The premium retires. `grep` is enough.

## The molt-breach and shed-prep plan

**Three artifacts retire, and each follows the law for what it is.**

### 1. `waymark-ladders.md` -- molt-breach to a living mutant

The rule page is one of the longest in `.claude/rules/`. A living mutant replaces it at a fresh
stamp, carrying only what the tree still needs:

- The warm-string table above (the translation from old names to new)
- The `waymark_rung_stamp_map.tsv` pointer (the translation from old rungs to stamps)
- The accrete-never-break clause (dated testimony keeps every word it wrote)
- A short account of why the system retired

The fossil -- the current `waymark-ladders.md` with its full registry table, derivation ceremony,
and collision history -- goes to `construction/SHRED_PREP.md` Class M (mitra shed prep), bannered
on its face, every living citer repointed.

### 2. `construction/waymark-registry.kyri` -- molt-breach to a sealed archive

The registry is SHA3-512-sealed and carries every draw this tree ever made. It is a record rather
than a living instrument. The sealed file moves to `construction/archive/`, bannered with its
retirement stamp and the reason. Its Class M row names what the cut asks: the derivation ceremony,
the word-list pin, and the derivation witness all retire with it.

### 3. `waymark-ascending-numbers.md` -- stays, with an update

The ascending-number ban stays in force -- the numbers are still banned. The rule page's
"Waymarks themselves stay" section updates to reflect the full retirement: waymarks themselves also
retire from living prose, alongside their ascending numbers.

## What the retirement preserves

**Dated testimony keeps every word it wrote.** Session logs, dated design essays, and folded shelves
carrying `lotus-audio`, `glow-language`, `lotus-audio` stay exactly as written until the deep debride reaches them.
After the deep debride, they are gone from history.

**The waymark-rung-to-stamp map stays.** `tools/fixtures/w/waymark_rung_stamp_map.tsv` is a
translation table, not a living usage. It maps each rung to its one-clock stamp so the conversion
has a mechanical source of truth. It stays readable after the deep debride, since the stamps are
what the tree actually filed by.

**The census instruments stay.** `waymark_ascending_census_scan.sh` and `waymark_rung_drift_scan.sh`
remain as the measurement and enforcement of the ascending-number ban. After the deep debride they
should read zero on all three tiers.

## Why this is right

A name owes everything to two readers: the person who meets it on their first day, and the one who
types it on their ten-thousandth. `lotus-audio` serves both where `lotus-audio` served neither. The
collision-proofing was real and the claims were zero, and a tree that has asked the collision question
six times in sixteen months can answer it with a grep. The system served this tree well in the chapter
that grew it. The chapter that outgrew it deserves warm names.
