# Bloom Guides -- Operational Manuals

**Style:** Bhakta at the Door setting, with Radiant warmth
**Status:** Living
**Room:** vision

---

These guides walk a reader through the daily operations of a food business
that uses Grain as its record-keeping system. Each guide assumes no prior
experience with Grain and explains every step from first principles.

## Guides

- [Farm management](farm-management.md) -- daily operations, seasonal
  planning, record-keeping for a biocyclic vegan farm
- [Factory operations](factory-operations.md) -- processing, quality
  control, batch tracking for a value-added food facility
- [Distribution operations](distribution-operations.md) -- logistics,
  cold chain, delivery coordination for a regional food hub
