# Bloom Ecology -- The Living Farm

**Style:** Bhakta at the Door setting, with Radiant warmth
**Status:** Living
**Room:** vision

---

This room holds the ecological foundation that gives Grain its purpose. The
curriculum's ten stages teach the machine; these pages teach what the machine
is for.

## Pages

- [Whole-system farming](whole-system-farming.md) -- Helen Atthowe's
  perennial systems, biocyclic vegan agriculture, soil as infrastructure
- [Energy and fuel](energy-and-fuel.md) -- veganic biodiesel, solar, the
  energy a small farm can produce for itself
- [Water and stewardship](water-and-stewardship.md) -- closed-loop water
  systems, constructed wetlands, responsible irrigation

## How these pages relate to the curriculum

Stage 8 (Ecology and Stewardship) introduces whole-system thinking and names
biocyclic vegan agriculture as Grain's ecological foundation. These pages go
deeper into each subject, giving a reader who wants to understand the farming
side of Grain a place to learn at their own pace.

Every page here follows the same discipline as the curriculum: Bhakta at the
Door setting, first principles in order, one new idea at a time, and every
claim grounded in what can be observed and measured.
