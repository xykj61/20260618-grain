Bloom is where a person learns what Grain is and how to use it. The name is a
seed that opens -- a bloom is what a grain becomes when the conditions are right,
and this platform exists to create those conditions for every learner who arrives.

## The ten stages

The curriculum follows a path from first encounter to full participation:

1. **Meeting the Dragons** -- What Grain is, and the two dragons that guard it
   (the Guardian Dragon of safety, the Companion Dragon of joy)
2. **Roots and Trees** -- The kernel as a living tree, rooted in bounded safety
3. **Memory as Garden** -- Allocation bounds, the garden arena, explicit widths
4. **Orchestra of Processes** -- Caravan supervision, dependent processes,
   graceful starts and stops
5. **Garden of Files** -- Mantra weave, Amphora seals, content-addressed storage
6. **Rivers of Networks** -- Comlink wire, sealed datagrams, the Noise handshake
7. **Treasure of Identity** -- Kumara naming, Monocypher signatures, SHA3
   for identity (Blake2s for wire protocol where speed on constrained hardware matters)
8. **Ecology and Stewardship** -- Whole-system care, soil as investment,
   biocyclic vegan principles
9. **Right to Repair** -- Open source, named errors, bounded complexity,
   the civic duty of building things that can be fixed
10. **The Technical Vision** -- All ten pieces in one picture: the cooperative
    farm, the Pond network, and the machine that belongs to its owner

Each stage is a self-contained lesson that stands on its own and connects to the
stages around it. A reader may begin at any stage, though stages 1 through 3
are where every newcomer starts.

## How to read this

Start with Stage 1 in `curriculum/`. Each stage opens with a plain description
of what it teaches, walks through the concepts with concrete examples, and
closes with something to try. The ecology and civic shelves deepen the themes
of Stages 8 and 9. The demos shelf puts the network stages (5 through 7) into
practice.

## Inspirations, thanked and siloed

Bloom draws from several teachers, each thanked in `gratitude/` and siloed into
Grain's own voice per `context/SILO_TECHNIQUE.md`:

- **DJINN's grain-school and grain-atlas** -- the interactive classroom and atlas
  interfaces, the Threshing Floor, Kyri teaching at a blackboard
- **The Guardian Dragon school** -- DJINN's ten-stage curriculum using
  storytelling metaphors for every layer of an operating system
- **Helen Atthowe** -- whole-system ecological thinking, the biocyclic vegan
  discipline, thirty years of perennial research
- **The grain-lineage-silo** -- six design principles this project carries
  forward from its own research

Each is understood, abstracted, and re-expressed in Grain's own words. The
originals live in their own honored rooms.
