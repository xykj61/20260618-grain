## What this tutorial teaches

Terrarium puts Grain's whole system under glass. The Bloom tab is where
that system teaches itself -- ten curriculum stages, ecology pages,
operational guides, and live network demos, all navigable from the same
window where you run witnesses and read ledgers.

This tutorial shows you how to find, read, and connect the learning
material inside Terrarium.

## What the Bloom tab holds

When you open the Bloom tab in Terrarium, you see five shelves:

1. **Curriculum** -- ten stages, from meeting the Guardian Dragon to the
   full technical vision. Start at Stage 1 and work through in order,
   or jump to the stage that matches what you are working on today.

2. **Ecology** -- whole-system farming, water stewardship, energy,
   materials, and biocyclic vegan principles. These pages deepen
   Stage 8 (Ecology and Stewardship).

3. **Guides** -- operational manuals for the farm, the factory, the
   distribution network, and the staff who run them. These pages
   deepen Stage 9 (Right to Repair) with practical daily operations.

4. **Demos** -- nine walkthroughs of a live 14-galaxy Pond/Puddle test
   network: galaxy registry, Kumara identity, Comlink sessions,
   Mycelium consensus, MUR token transfers, and Surf social. These
   pages put Stages 5, 6, and 7 into practice.

5. **Essays** -- four design essays on the ideas behind Grain's
   architecture: the moonshot, the small machine, vegan technology,
   and the cooperative farm.

## How to navigate

Each shelf is a folder inside `bloom/`. From the Bloom tab:

- **Tap a stage title** to open it. The stage opens in a reading pane
  with the full lesson, "Something to try" exercise, and a closing
  thought.
- **Follow a "Related demos" link** at the bottom of Stages 6 and 7
  to jump straight to the network walkthroughs that illustrate those
  concepts.
- **Return to the shelf** by tapping the back arrow or the Bloom tab
  icon.

The five shelves are independent. You do not need to read ecology before
opening a demo, or finish the curriculum before browsing the guides.
Each page declares its own style and stands on its own.

## Connecting Bloom to the other tabs

The other Terrarium tabs show the system running. Bloom explains what
you are seeing:

- **Terminal tab** -- you watch a witness prove itself. The curriculum
  explains what a witness is (Stage 2) and what bounded allocation
  means (Stage 3).
- **Dimeroll dashboard** -- you see transactions, inventory, and cash
  flow. The guides explain how the farm records its work
  (`farm-management.md`), and the ecology pages explain why the farm
  is designed the way it is.
- **Queries and Mantra** -- you ask the records a question. The demos
  walk through each query type on a live network.

Bloom is the tab that turns observation into understanding.

## Something to try

Open Stage 1 in the curriculum. Read it through. Then switch to the
Terminal tab and run any witness. Notice how the concepts from the
lesson -- safety, bounded allocation, named errors -- appear in the
witness output. The curriculum is describing what the Terminal tab is
showing you.
