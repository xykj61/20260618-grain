# Colloquium -- the civic-legislative module

**Status:** Living -- born `20261111` on Keaton's word
**Room:** vision -- civic legislation, patent analysis, policy drafting in Civic Style
**Style:** Gauge at the Field setting, with Radiant warmth
**Register:** Civic Style (`context/CIVIC_STYLE.md`)

Colloquium is where Grain reasons about law and public policy. Each bill in this room follows Civic Style's first principle: name the actual outcome, name what the reward measures, and confirm the two stay aligned under every foreseeable condition.

The name is a gathering for discussion -- a clear, warm word a newcomer grasps at once.

## The founding document

[`bills/automobile-patent-nullification.md`](bills/automobile-patent-nullification.md) -- a bill arguing that automobile patents should be nullified to move cars from consumer goods toward public safety utilities, funded through sovereign currency issuance with a federal automotive safety guarantee, and governed by certification roles whose rewards track provable reductions in defect and breakdown rates.

## What Colloquium is, and what it is beside

Colloquium is born fresh. It inherits nothing from Mandate (now Tablecloth's vector store) and nothing from any other module. The governance aspirations once envisioned for a Mandate expansion -- litigation, legislation proposals, federal contracts -- find their home here, under a name that fits.

Tablecloth holds the store and artifact database. Colloquium holds the law. The two may speak to each other -- a bill's evidentiary record could live in Tablecloth's content-addressed store -- yet each keeps its own room and its own discipline.

## Civic Style, in one line

Every policy rewards something. The whole of good design rests on a single question: what, exactly?
