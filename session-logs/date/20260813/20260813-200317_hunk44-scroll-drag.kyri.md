think The crux is the carried remainder: a touch device streams small pixel deltas, so a naive floor(delta/cell) loses a slow drag entirely (three pixels at a time over a ten-pixel cell never scrolls). A signed residual accumulates the sub-cell leftover between events, emitting a whole line only when the running total crosses a cell.
